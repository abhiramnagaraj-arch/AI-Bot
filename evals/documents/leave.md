# Synthetic Employee Leave Policy

These are fictional policies created only for testing.

Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave requests must reach the manager at least 5 working days before leave starts.

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried into the next calendar year.

Employees receive 7 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Unpaid personal leave is limited to 10 working days per calendar year and requires manager approval.
