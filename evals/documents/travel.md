# Synthetic Business Travel Policy

These are fictional policies created only for testing.

The hotel reimbursement ceiling is USD 180 per night, excluding mandatory local taxes.

Meals are reimbursed up to USD 45 per day when travelling on approved business.

Travel receipts must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends.

Business travel requires manager approval at least 7 calendar days before departure.

Flights must be booked in economy class. Business class requires director approval and flight duration over 8 hours.
